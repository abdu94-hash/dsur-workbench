DSUR section-level QC checklist

The interactive version of Appendix B of Writing the DSUR. This checklist collects the chapter checklists in one place, grouped by the stage of the DSUR they check. Use it on the review draft and again on the final (Chapter 15). Start with the five high-yield checks, which find most of the errors that reach assessors and inspectors. Mark each check Pass, Issue or N/A, add a comment where needed, and print a dated record for the DSUR file.
