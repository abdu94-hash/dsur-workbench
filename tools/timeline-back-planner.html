DSUR timeline back-planner

Enter a data lock point (DLP). The planner fills in the calendar date of every milestone in the 60-day model from Writing the DSUR (Chapter 3, 3.2; Appendix C.1). Day 0 is the data lock point; only the day-60 limit comes from ICH E2F (§2.2). Adjust the other days to the size of your program, but keep scope, templates and output shells settled before the data lock point.

    Planning notes (Appendix C.2)

    • Work back from the earliest deadline. When the 60th day falls on a weekend or public holiday, submit before it, not after. Where a region's deadline is earlier than day 60, rebuild the calendar backward from that deadline (set the day numbers accordingly).
    • Protect the key messages review. It is the milestone most often dropped under pressure, and dropping it costs the most (Chapter 17).
    • Set the late-breaking cut-off at the start of the consolidated review, and name its owner (Chapter 8).
    • Keep ten days between sign-off and the deadline for publishing, regional packaging and problems.
